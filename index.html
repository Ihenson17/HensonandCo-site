Common questions

Is there a contract?

No. Everything is month-to-month. You can pause or cancel anytime — we'd rather earn your business every month than lock you in.

Who owns the content?

You do. Every caption, graphic, and post we create is yours to keep, repost, or reuse however you'd like — even if you cancel.

How much time does this take from me?

Usually under 15 minutes a month. You review and approve the content calendar before anything goes live — that's it.

What do you need from me to get started?

A few recent photos, your Instagram or Google Business login (or you can post it yourself), and a quick sense of your business and customers. We handle the rest.

How fast can I see a sample?

Send us your business info and we'll turn around three sample posts, free, usually within a couple of days.

Want to see what this looks like for your business?

Send us a link to your Instagram or website and we'll put together three sample posts — free, no commitment.
